JWST Telescope Data

Exoplanet K2-18b Water Signatures

Advanced spectroscopy from the James Webb Space Telescope has captured carbon-bearing molecules in the atmosphere of a habitable-zone exoplanet.

Located 124 light-years away, K2-18b orbits a cool dwarf star. While exciting, scientists caution that detecting these molecules does not automatically confirm biological life, but it narrows down prime targets for search.
Planetary Science

Europa's Subsurface Dynamics

New gravitational and magnetic data models suggest the ice shell of Jupiter's moon Europa may feature active liquid pockets near the crust.

Europa possesses an internal global ocean containing more water than all of Earth's oceans combined, kept liquid by intense tidal flexing from Jupiter's massive gravitational pull.
