Artemis Campaign

NASA's ongoing effort to return humans to the Moon, establishing a sustainable base for future crewed missions to Mars.

Target: Lunar South Pole Region
Objective: Long-term habitat testing and ice resource extraction.
Partner Agencies: NASA, ESA, JAXA, CSA
Status: Active on Surface

Mars Perseverance Rover

The robotic scientist cruising Jezero Crater, collecting samples, and hunting for signs of ancient microbial life.

Location: Jezero Crater, Mars
Key Tool: Sample caching system for a future Mars Sample Return mission.
Milestone: Successfully tested atmospheric oxygen production via MOXIE.
Status: En Route

ESA JUICE Mission

The Jupiter Icy Moons Explorer is journeying toward the Jovian system to investigate Ganymede, Callisto, and Europa.

Launch Date: April 2023
Arrival Year: 2031
Focus: Assessing whether Jupiter's subsurface oceans could harbor habitable environments.
